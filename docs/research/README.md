## Handling evidence

Assign a business/research owner, a rights reviewer, and a restricted evidence location before outreach or collection. A document template is not consent, a license, or authority to contact people. Verify authorization for each intended use before execution.

- Keep agreements, contact details, credentials, participant identities, real VIN/linkage keys, licensed provider records, photos, and invoices outside Git in the approved restricted location. Only these blank templates and explicitly synthetic examples belong in this repository.
- Use opaque evidence/case IDs in working manifests; keep any identity/contact lookup separately restricted. Pseudonymization does not grant publication rights. Never turn private manifest paths into public links.
- Record the collection purpose, actual rights basis, allowed audience/uses, evidence owner, effective/expiry dates, retention/deletion policy and reviewer. Unknown rights deny that use; no arbitrary default retention period is proposed.
- Permit only the minimum necessary fields and approved staff. Record note-taking and recording consent separately; contributors cannot grant auction-photo rights they do not own. Do not upload any collected material to an AI service in this sprint.
- At withdrawal, agreement expiry or revocation, stop the affected use; identify source, derived copies and backups covered by the policy; verify required deletion or restricted retention and record completion. Do not promise deletion terms that conflict with an applicable documented obligation.
- Publish only rights-reviewed aggregate findings and synthetic examples. Redaction alone does not permit publishing licensed content.

Report pending and excluded evidence honestly. At preparation time the verified counts are **0 real identity-study links, 0 accepted repair examples, and 0 interviews**. Future owners update private working records and a reviewed aggregate report after actual execution; they must not populate these repository templates with restricted material.

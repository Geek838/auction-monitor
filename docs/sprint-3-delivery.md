## Meaning of preview completion

`COMPLETED` means a validated seeded mock workflow completed. It does not mean a vehicle passed an inspection, no damage exists, a model ran, or a safety assessment was made. `PARTIAL` must identify excluded evidence. Missing/invalid/unauthorized assets must not acquire another scenario's findings. A target change requires new resolution; a report must never remain under a different listing or stale record/event card.

Inspection provenance is distinct from listing metadata provenance. A SIMULATED listing can carry a MOCK preview only through the documented fixture binding and visible labels. Image authorship and display permission do not imply AI inference or model-training permission. Fixture text describes drawn examples, not real Camry condition; current source-age and historical labels remain meaningful limitations.

The PO/copy review covered the authored-image captions/manifest and fixed `MockInspectionEngine` findings/checks. Observations explicitly describe drawn marks; the bumper-attachment suspicion is an inspection question with confirmation, not a damage diagnosis. Structure, SRS/airbags, ADAS, cooling, suspension and mechanical condition remain unknown with qualified-inspection recommendations. The rear/no-highlight case does not imply absence of damage. This is a review of deterministic fixture wording, not a qualified automotive assessment or proof that a general-purpose text validator can recognize every unsafe statement.
